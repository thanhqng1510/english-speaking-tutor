# Audio & Digital Frequency Resources

## Knowledge

- [MDN Web Docs: Web Audio API - Basic Concepts](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Web_Audio_API/Basic_concepts_behind_Web_Audio_API)
  Clear explanation of audio sampling, sample rates, and audio frames in browser environments. Use for: understanding how web audio nodes and sample rates work.
- [Microsoft Learn: Audio Formats for Speech Recognition](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-services/speech-service/how-to-use-audio-input-streams)
  Official specifications for 16 kHz 16-bit mono PCM in speech engines. Use for: understanding why speech recognition APIs use 16 kHz.
- [Wikipedia: Audio Frequency & Human Hearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audio_frequency)
  Fundamental physical reference for acoustic waves and human pitch range (20 Hz to 20,000 Hz).

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [Web Audio Slack Community](https://web-audio-slack.herokuapp.com/)
  Active community of developers building audio apps on the web. Use for: real-world browser audio implementation questions.
