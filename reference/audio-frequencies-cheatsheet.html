Quick Reference Guide

Audio & Computing Frequency Cheat Sheet

A one-page reference for Hz, event timers, pitch, and sample rates.

1. The Formula

1 Hertz (Hz) = 1 event or cycle per second

1 Kilohertz (kHz) = 1,000 Hertz = 1,000 cycles per second

2. Frequency Across the 3 Application Domains

Domain Typical Values What It Measures Why It Matters in Our App
Software Event Timer 4 Hz vs 60 Hz How many times code checks the time per second. 4 Hz (every 250ms) causes karaoke word lag. 60 Hz (every 16.6ms) gives buttery-smooth word highlights.
Human Voice Pitch 85 Hz – 255 Hz How fast vocal cords physically vibrate. Used for intonation & pitch curve charts (e.g. rising pitch on questions). Male voices ~100 Hz, female voices ~200 Hz.
Digital Audio Sample Rate 16 kHz vs 44.1 kHz How many audio snapshot numbers the computer records per second. Speech models (Azure) demand 16 kHz (16,000 samples/sec). Captures all human speech consonants while keeping files small.

3. The Nyquist Theorem Rule of Thumb

To accurately capture an audio frequency of X Hz, your digital sample rate must be at least 2 × X Hz.

  • Human speech clarity reaches up to 8 kHz.
  • Therefore, speech sample rate = 2 × 8 kHz = 16 kHz.
