# Mission: Audio and Digital Frequency Fundamentals for Speech Apps

## Why
Understand what frequency (Hz, kHz) means across software events, human voice pitch, and digital audio sampling, so the learner can make confident technical and product design decisions when building the English speaking tutor web app.

## Success looks like
- Accurately explain what "Hertz (Hz)" measures in any context (events per second, acoustic wave cycles, and digital audio samples).
- Understand why a 4 Hz browser event creates karaoke highlighting lag, while 60 Hz eliminates it.
- Understand the difference between sound pitch (e.g. 100 Hz male voice vs 250 Hz female voice) and digital audio sample rate (e.g. 16 kHz vs 44.1 kHz).
- Confidently evaluate speech API requirements (e.g. why Azure AI Speech requires 16 kHz Linear PCM).

## Constraints
- Ground all concepts in real-world analogies and the specific features of the English speaking tutor app.
- Avoid abstract mathematics or complex calculus.

## Out of scope
- Analog circuit engineering, fast Fourier transform (FFT) complex mathematical proofs, filter electrical schematics.
