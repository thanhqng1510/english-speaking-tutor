# English Speaking Tutor

A web app where learners practice speaking English aloud from text they supply, with guidance on pronunciation and prosody.

## Language

### Prosody

**Chunk**:
A short run of words a speaker says as one unit, between natural pauses, carrying a single main emphasis.
_Avoid_: Thought group, breath group, breath unit, phrase; and using "chunk" for pieces of audio or streamed data (call those frames or buffers)

**Focus Word**:
The single most emphasised word in a Chunk, where the main pitch movement falls.
_Avoid_: Nuclear stress word, main stress word, key word

**Word Stress**:
The emphasis on one syllable inside a single word (the PHO in PHOtograph); distinct from the Focus Word of a Chunk.
_Avoid_: Main stress (when meaning the Focus Word)

**Chunking**:
The process of splitting learner text into an ordered sequence of Chunks.
_Avoid_: Segmentation, thought-group chunking
